# RetroBard privacy notice

RetroBard runs only on your computer. It captures only the game window you link and reads its
dialogue box. It checks only the skip keys and gamepad buttons in your profile, and only while
the game window is focused. To help you pick the game, the Setup tab lists your open windows'
titles; they are not saved. With ElevenLabs voices on, the dialogue text is sent to ElevenLabs
using your own API key, under your ElevenLabs account's terms. ElevenLabs may use it to improve
its models unless you opt out in your ElevenLabs account (Terms and Privacy > Data use).
With a custom voice server on, the dialogue text is sent to the server address you entered:
a server on your own computer keeps it there, an online service follows its own terms.
Spoken lines are cached on your PC in %APPDATA%\RetroBard\cache; you can clear them on the
Options tab. RetroBard has no accounts, telemetry or servers, and sends nothing to the developer.
