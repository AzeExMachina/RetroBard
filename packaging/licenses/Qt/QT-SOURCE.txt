Qt in RetroBard
===============

RetroBard uses Qt {qt_version} through PySide6 {pyside_version} and shiboken6
(Qt for Python), under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 3
(LGPL-3.0, see LGPL-3.0.txt in this folder; it refers to GPL-3.0.txt).

Source code of these exact versions:
- Qt: https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/
- PySide6 and shiboken6: https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/

Qt is not built into RetroBard.exe. Its DLLs, plugins and the PySide6 modules are
separate files in the _internal/PySide6 folder next to RetroBard.exe, and you may
replace them with your own build of the same Qt and PySide6 versions.
